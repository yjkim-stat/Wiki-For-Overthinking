# abstention

<!-- auto:begin -->

Declining to answer when no answer is warranted, treated by the sources as a capability separate from answering correctly. In the referring-expression work it is the required output when the described object is absent, and models fail it by emitting hallucinated bounding boxes because training contained no negative samples. In the proverb benchmark it is the harder condition by construction: models complete proverbs successfully but cannot detect that no option is correct, even while naming the right ending in their reasoning. Both sources find abstention degrades independently of task accuracy, so a model can know the answer and still fail to withhold one.
