# attention head

<!-- auto:begin -->

An individual attention mechanism within a layer, and across 3 sources the unit at which the archive's mechanistic results are most often stated. Its uses divide by what is claimed. As a locus of function: latent counts are computed and stored in the final item representations of each part, transferred to intermediate steps via dedicated heads, and aggregated at the final stage -- a mechanism located at head granularity. As a source of directions: the foundational steering method mines vectors from the most discriminative heads, which is exactly the selection rule the archive's inverted-vector result shows can install the opposite of the intended effect. And as a search space: one source generalises causal tracing from one component at a time to joint subsets, relaxing the combinatorial selection into a continuous one over soft interventions, on the argument that heads act together.
