# emergent behaviour

<!-- auto:begin -->

Behaviour that appears without being trained for directly, which the sources treat as both the appeal and the reliability problem of outcome-based RL. One argues the reasoning behaviours RL elicits incidentally are unpredictable in timing and consistency, and replaces them with explicit alignment to logical primitives. Another reports emergence in an adversarial setting, where a trained planner discovers decoy avoidance and adaptive state prioritization that static heuristics never produce. A survey organizes such findings under unintended behaviours alongside training dynamics and reasoning mechanisms. The shared position is that emergence is real and that relying on it is a design weakness.
