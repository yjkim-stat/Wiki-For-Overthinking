# linear separability

<!-- auto:begin -->

Whether two classes can be divided by a hyperplane in activation space, and across 4 sources the property that licenses probing and is repeatedly shown not to license anything else. The corpus's central instance: concept recoverability and concept influence come apart -- one bias analysis finds sparse autoencoders improving the first while attenuating the second, which is the detection-versus-control gap stated in a different vocabulary. A second: a simpler linear probe is consistently stronger than a kernel-derived concept vector on a human-annotated benchmark, so more expressive readouts do not automatically buy more. A third is architectural: which steering strategy stays stable depends on the conditioning pathway, so separability at a site does not imply intervenability at that site. The archive's operative position is that separability is well established for reading and says nothing about writing, and that a probe's success is compatible with its having latched onto lexical surface patterns unless a null control says otherwise.
