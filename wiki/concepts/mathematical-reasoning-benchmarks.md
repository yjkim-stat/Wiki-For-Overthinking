# mathematical reasoning benchmarks

<!-- auto:begin -->

The umbrella category of competition-math evaluation sets (AIME, AMC, MATH, MATH-500, HMMT, GPQA) the archive's sources use to measure test-time-compute and reasoning-length methods. BG-MCTS's budget-aware tree search and 'Reasoning Models Hallucinate More' both evaluate their methods on this class of benchmark.

- [Reasoning Models Hallucinate More: Factuality-Aware Reinforcement Learning for Large Reasoning Models](../../archive/papers/2025/title-99b7eabb818cb959/summary.md) — Shows that RL fine-tuning for reasoning increases hallucination and proposes FSPO, a step-wise factuality-verified RL objective that reduces it while improving accuracy.
