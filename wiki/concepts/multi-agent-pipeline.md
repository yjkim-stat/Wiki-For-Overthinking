# multi-agent pipeline

<!-- auto:begin -->

Several model instances with distinct roles passing work between them, and across 3 sources a design whose advantage the archive finds is narrow and conditional. The measured case: a persona-conditioned deliberation architecture whose recall advantage is not reproduced by best-of-n sampling from the same model, concentrates entirely in the cases where monolithic inference fails, and reverses on the easiest quartile -- which is the right way to report such a result and the pattern to expect. The cost side is measured too: implicit state can survive session boundaries, and whether injected contamination decays depends on the kind of contamination rather than on model scale or deployment, with in-context self-reflection ranging from -14 to +45 percent across models. And the training side inherits the archive's credit-assignment problem, since a multi-turn pipeline receives one terminal signal for many agents' decisions.
