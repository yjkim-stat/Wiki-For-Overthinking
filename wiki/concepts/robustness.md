# robustness

<!-- auto:begin -->

Whether performance survives changes that should not matter, and the sources make it a claim about what benchmarks are measuring. One argues that reasoning models' Theory of Mind gains are increased robustness to prompt variation and task perturbation rather than a new capability, reading benchmark improvement as variance reduction. The other measures it across functionally equivalent code implementations and finds a substantial gap, meaning predictions depend on surface form rather than on execution semantics. Both establish that single-prompt, single-implementation evaluation reports a point estimate of something that varies, and both treat the variation as the finding rather than as noise.
