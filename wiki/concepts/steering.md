# steering

<!-- auto:begin -->

Directing a model toward a target behaviour by intervening on its internals at inference. The two sources here differ in scope: one builds multi-layer steering vectors from sparse-autoencoder features selected by co-activation and decoder-direction alignment, reporting it as the only method that consistently preserves fluency where single-point interventions fail entirely on sycophancy and refusal; the other places steering as the intervention half of a Locate-Steer-Improve pipeline categorized by the interpretable object being acted on. Neither tests steering on reasoning itself, so whether a reasoning process is steerable the way an attribute like toxicity is remains open in this archive.
