# AIME24-25

<!-- auto:begin -->

AIME24-25 (combined 2024/2025 AIME competition problems) is used in these sources as a hard math-reasoning benchmark for mechanistic and distillation studies: the Self-Reading Patterns paper reports SRQ-driven activation steering achieving up to +6.6 accuracy points on AIME24-25 (using vectors transferred from MATH500), and Pru-CoT evaluates its gradient-based step-importance pruning distillation method on AIME24/25 among its four benchmarks, showing the largest single-benchmark gain there.

## Appears in

- [How Do Answer Tokens Read Reasoning Traces? Self-Reading Patterns in Thinking LLMs for Quantitative Reasoning](../../archive/papers/2026/doi-10-18653-v1-2026-findings-acl-1507/summary.md) — Analyzing how answer tokens attend back to reasoning tokens ('self-reading') in thinking LLMs reveals a stable, structured 'benign self-reading' pattern strongly correlated with correctness -- a forward-drifting attention centroid plus persistent focus on key semantic anchors -- interpreted as internal certainty, versus diffuse/irregular attention in incorrect solutions; a training-free Self-Reading Quality (SRQ) score built from this pattern is used to select contrastive samples for activation-steering vectors that consistently improve accuracy (up to 2.6pp) across three models, three steering mechanisms, and multiple quantitative-reasoning benchmarks including out-of-domain transfer.
- [Pru-CoT: Towards Efficient Reasoning Distillation via Pruning Chain-of-Thought](../../archive/papers/2026/doi-10-18653-v1-2026-findings-acl-1684/summary.md) — Pru-CoT quantifies each reasoning step's causal contribution via gradient-based optimization of a learnable soft-masking weight on a frozen student LLM (masking a step toward a filler-token embedding and seeing how much the prediction loss spikes), then uses an LLM-guided pruning agent to synthesize a concise, logically-coherent chain anchored on the high-weight steps -- beating token-level, prompt-based, and search-based pruning baselines, improving DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B's average accuracy 2.4% while cutting tokens, and running 4.4x faster than the strongest search-based baseline (A*-Thought).
