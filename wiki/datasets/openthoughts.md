# OpenThoughts

<!-- auto:begin -->

A corpus of reasoning questions with traces, used in this archive in two unrelated roles. One source uses it as a training set and reports a 4x efficiency gain over standard training on it; the other uses it as a question pool spanning nine sources -- mathematics, coding, biology, chemistry, physics and commonsense -- split 540 optimization and 360 test, and notes that only the question text is used with all answers, labels and metadata excluded. Neither source characterises its size or construction beyond that.
