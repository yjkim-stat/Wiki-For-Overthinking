# What we have settled

## Reasoning Evaluation

- **Established** — Internal signals that predict answer correctness do not transfer across tasks, even when the structural geometry they are computed from does, so a correctness detector validated on one benchmark says little about another.
  - _Three independently formulated late-layer signals agree on the shape. Step-specific representation structure transfers across datasets at probe accuracy above 0.85, while the correctness predictor built on the same activations falls from 0.87 in distribution to 0.73 on MATH-500, 0.64 on MMLU and 0.60 on the same dataset in a freeform prompt format. A trajectory-geometry classifier needs a per-domain reference set and recovers cross-domain performance only after centroid alignment. A hidden-state norm signal has no universal cutoff at all -- a high norm on GSM8K is a low norm on GPQA -- and replacing its adaptive per-decode threshold with a fixed one collapses AIME24 accuracy from 70.0 to 23.3. The practical rule for the archive: treat any reported AUROC for an internal correctness signal as in-distribution until shown otherwise, and require calibration data per task._

## Reasoning Faithfulness

- **Established** — The content of a model's intermediate reasoning tokens is often not what produces its answer: traces can be swapped between problems, or latent thoughts deleted outright, with the answer unchanged.
  - _Established causally by two independent papers read together, in settings where the trace can be checked mechanically rather than read. Transformers trained on A* maze traces keep their solution accuracy when trained on traces swapped between instances, with accuracy high at both 0% and 100% swapped and dipping only in the middle. Fine-tuned latent reasoners on ProsQA lose at most 1.0 point when every latent token is deleted, and entity probing shows why -- the target entity dominates their belief from the first latent step, so the model solves the problem in one forward pass and copies the answer through the latent slots. Both were measured on tasks deliberately built to require multi-step or parallel exploration, which is what makes the negative result strong rather than a limitation of the setting. This does not say intermediate tokens never help; it says their helping is not explained by their content._
