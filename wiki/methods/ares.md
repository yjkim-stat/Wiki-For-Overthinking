# ARES

<!-- auto:begin -->

Two unrelated systems in this archive are both called ARES, and this entry covers both rather than one. The first is an LLM-agent RTL optimizer that raises the per-call reasoning effort only after progress stalls and reports the normalized dollar cost of every call next to the power-area-delay figure of merit. The second trains multimodal reasoning models to spend exploration effort in proportion to problem difficulty, using sliding-window token entropy as the signal for when and how much to explore. They share only the name and the general commitment to spending reasoning effort where it pays; do not read any claim about one as evidence about the other.
