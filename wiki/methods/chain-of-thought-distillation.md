# chain of thought distillation

<!-- auto:begin -->

Training a model to produce long-form reasoning by fitting it to reasoning traces generated by a stronger model, rather than by rewarding its own attempts. The sources use the term for the transfer itself and disagree about what transfers. DRP treats the trace as content that must be matched to the student: a teacher decomposes and prunes the traces by mathematical skill before distilling them back, on the argument that a chain exceeding the student's capacity does not help it. The trajectory analysis measures the effect representationally and finds it narrower than expected -- a distilled variant of Llama 3.1 8B shares its step-specific representation structure with the base model it was distilled from, and what distillation changes is the layer depth at which termination-related structure becomes linearly accessible, while intermediate steps become somewhat less separable. Taken together the sources describe a procedure that reliably moves behaviour without necessarily installing new representational organization.
