# Chain-of-Thought Distillation

<!-- auto:begin -->

Training a smaller student model on chain-of-thought traces generated by a larger teacher. Both sources treat it less as a single technique than as a pipeline whose outcome is determined by what enters the training data: DC-CoT is a benchmark that isolates the separate effects of data augmentation, data selection and data mixing across teacher models, student models and reasoning domains, while LIMOPro edits the traces before training, scoring each reasoning step by its effect on answer confidence (PIR) and pruning only low-importance verification and error-correction steps. LIMOPro's result is the one that matters for reasoning length: the concision of the student is inherited from the traces it was shown, so removing redundant verification steps from the data produces a more concise student without costing accuracy.
