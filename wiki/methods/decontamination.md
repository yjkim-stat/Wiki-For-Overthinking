# decontamination

<!-- auto:begin -->

Removing training items that overlap evaluation items, and across 4 sources a procedure the archive reads as a threshold choice with consequences rather than a hygiene step. The instances that report their own numbers are the useful ones. One audit runs three stages, flags one problem in an external benchmark, confirms it by inspection as a false positive, and reports maximum problem-level similarity to any training item at 0.84 with a median of 0.53 -- which is what makes its 20-to-28-point rationale-supervision result readable. Another decontaminates by cosine similarity at 0.90 and reports maximum retained similarities of 0.898 and 0.891, immediately below the cut, with means of 0.866 and 0.845; it attributes this to the structural density of synthetic mathematics corpora rather than to leakage and defends the choice with an answer-distinct retrieval control rather than by lowering the threshold. A third removes flagged overlaps at three levels -- problem, solution and skill card -- and retrains every reported model afterwards. The archive's reading is that decontamination establishes implausibility rather than absence, that the retained-similarity distribution is the number to report, and that a control distinguishing structural similarity from leakage is what makes a threshold defensible.

## Appears in

- [MathShikkha: A Controlled Study of Answer-Only and Chain-of-Thought Supervision for Bangla Mathematical Reasoning in Small Language Models](../../archive/papers/2026/arxiv-2608-08503/summary.md) — Compares chain-of-thought against answer-only supervision under a protocol where the two conditions differ in nothing but the training target, and finds the rationales buy nothing in-domain for strong backbones while buying 20 to 28 points out of domain -- with a human study attributing the measurable effect to language adherence and inspectability rather than to better reasoning.
- [SafeSceneReason: A Multimodal Reasoning Benchmark Connecting Industrial Hazards with Accident Knowledge](../../archive/papers/2026/arxiv-2608-09230/summary.md) — Builds an industrial-safety reasoning benchmark from two pipelines -- program execution over safety scene graphs, and evidence graphs extracted from real accident-investigation reports -- and shows that general multimodal capability does not transfer to it while a 9B model fine-tuned on its chain-of-thought split matches frontier systems.
- [Calibrating Post-Training Feature Shifts for LLM Data Contamination Detection](../../archive/papers/2026/arxiv-2608-10462/summary.md) — Calibrates feature-based data-contamination detectors against the systematic feature shifts that post-training introduces, by measuring how controlled prompt variants move scores on known non-members and then correcting only the recurring shift directions, with the gains concentrated at the low-false-positive operating point rather than in AUC.
- [ThinkRetrieve: Retrieval-Augmented Reasoning Traces for Test-Time Scaling](../../archive/papers/2026/arxiv-2608-10928/summary.md) — Injects a retrieved solved problem, with its full worked solution, into the middle of a reasoning model's own thinking trace at each step boundary, using the model's current intermediate answer as the retrieval query.
