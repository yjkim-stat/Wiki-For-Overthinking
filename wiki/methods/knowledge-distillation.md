# knowledge distillation

<!-- auto:begin -->

Training a smaller student model on data generated by a larger teacher. In this archive it appears mostly as a comparison arm rather than as a subject: one source contrasts it with self-improvement and with weak-to-strong improvement, finding a weak-cheap teacher beats a strong-expensive one by about 5 points under distillation, and another reports that small verifiers remain unreliable on memorisation-dependent checks even after distillation from larger verifiers. Its other role here is as the origin of the models the archive studies -- the R1-Distill family -- where sources repeatedly separate distilled from RL-trained checkpoints because the two behave differently under the same intervention.
