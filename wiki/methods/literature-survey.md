# literature survey

<!-- auto:begin -->

A review paper rather than a technique applied to models; it appears as an entity here because two archived sources are surveys of the same field and named it as their method. The two organize mechanistic interpretability incompatibly: one by object of study — training dynamics, reasoning mechanisms, unintended behaviours — and the other by intervention, as a Locate, Steer and Improve pipeline categorized by the interpretable object acted on. Both name the same gap, that the field's findings are not yet actionable or auditable. Two irreconcilable taxonomies of one area arriving together is itself a description of the field's state.
