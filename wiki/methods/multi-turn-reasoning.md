# multi-turn reasoning

<!-- auto:begin -->

Reasoning that unfolds across several exchanges with a user or an environment rather than in one response, which both sources identify as an under-measured regime. One builds a benchmark for it — 4 classes, 40 tasks, 3600 instances, fully automated construction and evaluation — and finds even cutting-edge reasoning models fall short. The other uses multi-turn reasoning as one of two attempted remedies for the finding that fewer than 25% of reasoning traces follow a given instruction. The archive is otherwise dominated by single-turn math and QA, so this pair marks a gap rather than filling it.
