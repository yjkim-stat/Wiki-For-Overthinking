# DeepSeek-V4-Flash

<!-- auto:begin -->

A frontier model appearing across 3 sources as an agent backbone and an evaluation subject. Its most consequential archived result is as the backbone for a diagnosis-guided recovery harness, where the same model goes from 40.13 to 91.94 percent on an embodied benchmark and from 18.32 to 74.90 on a multi-application one -- and where the matched-information rerun shows the gain belongs to the recovery interface rather than to privileged information. It also appears in contamination-mitigation evaluation and in a financial benchmark where unit and scale perturbations collapse the strongest models.
