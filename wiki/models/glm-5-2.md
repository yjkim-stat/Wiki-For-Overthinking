# GLM-5.2

<!-- auto:begin -->

A Z.ai reasoning model, used in this archive in three service roles rather than as an object of study. It is one of three reference models whose average unbudgeted output length defines the difficulty stratification of a shared-budget benchmark, it is one of that benchmark's six evaluated flagships, and it is one of three open-CoT targets in a chain-of-thought extraction study. In the shared-budget setting sources describe it as domain-sensitive: its allocation gaps and its response to external scheduling vary sharply by domain, which is why it was picked as the case testing whether a scheduler must adapt to the kind of runtime evidence a task provides.
