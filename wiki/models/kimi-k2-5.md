# Kimi-K2.5

<!-- auto:begin -->

A frontier model used by both sources as apparatus rather than as a subject, and in both the role is verification. One uses it as the independent verifier filtering candidate evidence boxes and rationales generated by a different large model, so it decides which supervision survives into a training corpus. The other evaluates it in a large-scale study of judge reliability. The pairing is worth keeping: a model that filters another model's annotations is setting the ceiling on what a student can learn, and the archive separately holds evidence that judge quality is prompt- and format-dependent.
