# Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct

<!-- auto:begin -->

An open-weight instruction-tuned model used across sources both as an evaluation subject in the POBs preference/opinion/belief test-time-compute study, and (per another archived source) as the model used to segment and label LRM reasoning traces by Bloom's Taxonomy cognitive level in a reasoning-trace cognitive-profiling study.

## Appears in

- [Cognitive Profiling of LRMs' Reasoning Traces Using Bloom's Taxonomy](../../archive/papers/2026/arxiv-2608-23205/summary.md) — The paper segments LRM reasoning traces into cognitive steps with Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct, labels each step with one of Bloom's six levels, and uses the resulting level proportions and 6x6 transition matrix to profile seven reasoning models and to predict solution correctness.
- [Think Again! The Effect of Test-Time Compute on Preferences, Opinions, and Beliefs of Large Language Models](../../archive/papers/2025/doi-10-18653-v1-2025-acl-industry-45/summary.md) — Introduces POBs, a 20-topic Likert-scale benchmark for LLM preferences/opinions/beliefs on controversial topics, finding models consistently lean progressive-collectivist (with newer versions more strongly and less consistently so), and that adding reasoning or self-reflection prompting gives only limited improvement to reliability, neutrality, or consistency.
