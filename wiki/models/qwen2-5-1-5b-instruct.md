# Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct

<!-- auto:begin -->

A small instruction-tuned model, and in both sources the checkpoint on which a claim is tested rather than developed. One includes it among 18 models where an entity's culture is linearly readable from the residual stream while generation emits it far less often. The other uses it for its whole real-model section: an emergent indirect-object circuit found by the field's own greedy patching search, on which the patching-versus-ablation gap measures 0.224 to 0.852 across five lexical instances, and against whose real weights a closed-form attention Jacobian bound is verified pointwise without a violation. Its value in both is the same — it is a genuinely pretrained model at a scale where a full circuit search is affordable, so a claim developed on synthetic networks can be checked somewhere it was not designed to hold.
